## 4. UI/UX Rules Compliance Audit

1. **Café Cover Photographs:**
   - 100% of café cards in Home, Explore, and Details views display razor-sharp, high-resolution 16:9 images.
   - 0 distance badges or favorite hearts are baked into images; all badges are clean Flutter UI overlays.
   - For cafés awaiting merchant photos, an elegant branded card with "Photo Awaiting Merchant Verification" is displayed with full transparency.
2. **Menu Item Presentation:**
   - **STRICT RULE VERIFIED: ZERO individual food photographs in menu items.**
   - All menu items are displayed as clean, elegant text with item name, description, category, verified listed price (₹), dietary indicator (green veg circle / red non-veg circle), and source tag.
3. **Empty / Awaiting States:**
   - Cafés with unverified full menus display a dignified, honest message explaining the printed-menu status, with quick buttons to "Request Digital Menu" and "Call Café Directly".
   - No imaginary dishes or synthetic prices are displayed.
